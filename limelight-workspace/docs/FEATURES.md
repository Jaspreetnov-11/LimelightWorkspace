# Limelight Workspace - features, benefits and why it matters

Source: the handbook in this folder (README, area pages, CHANGELOG, servers, phone-speed-app, PENDING) and `docs/_notes/`. Only features that exist today are listed. Things still being built or not yet tested are in **Coming next** at the end, or marked "(not yet tested live)" where they appear.

---

## 1. Summary

The Limelight Workspace (online at **limelightcorp.net**) is Limelight's own all-in-one office platform: one window, one login, and every tool the team uses to make government and ministry communication - video editing (**Edit**), design (**Design**), mail, calendar, meetings, documents, HR, overtime and salary (**Office**), a private AI (**Limelight AI**), team chat, an AI image maker and a shared video library. It runs on Limelight's own PCs and its own NAS, uses the HR account staff already have, and works on a computer, tablet or phone. For an agency that handles confidential ministry material and delivers video, design and social media on tight deadlines, it puts the work, the files and the client data in one place the company owns, cuts the number of paid tools and logins, and lets the team move from brief to finished output without leaving one app.

## 3. Area by area

### Home

- Greeting with live date and time, plus a daily Bhagavad Gita line (English with the Hindi).
- **Clock-in card**: selfie, Office / WFH / Field choice and location, as the HR app requires; running "Working · Xh Ym" timer and Clock out. On a phone: slide to clock in / slide to clock out.
- Short welcome animation after clocking in.
- **App cards** for Edit, Design, Office and Limelight AI, each with Live/Off status and a live figure (designs this week, new mails, an "Ask or create" AI box). A **Start** button for Office and Design if they are off.
- **Number tiles**: unread chats, today's tasks, attendance.
- **Lists**: Today feed (All / Chats / Tasks / Files), Meetings, Projects, Team updates - each row opens the exact chat, task, design or page.
- **Search everywhere** (Ctrl+K / Cmd+K): apps, Office pages, chats, people, designs, plus "Make an image / video with AI" from the typed text.
- Apps open filling the window under one slim bar and stay loaded while you switch; full screen keeps the bar.
- **14 colour themes** (Pure Black default, light themes, and a Custom 5-colour theme), remembered per device.
- Home opens fast: each data source is capped at 3 s and cached, so first load is about 0.2 s and later loads about 2 ms.

**Benefits**
- Time: one screen shows the day (tasks, meetings, mail, chats) instead of checking several apps.
- Control: clock-in uses the existing HR rules (selfie, location), so attendance stays in one system.
- Quality: a slow source shows "–" and never holds the page up.

**Why it matters**
- Staff start the day in one place and reach any tool in one click, which matters when a ministry asks for a change at short notice.

### Chats

- WhatsApp-style **1-to-1 chats and groups**; group admins rename, describe, add/remove members and make other admins.
- Send **photos and files up to 35 MB** (button, drag-and-drop or paste).
- Reply, **delete for everyone** (own messages), read ticks, typing, online dot, unread counts, desktop notifications.
- **Forward** any message (marked "Forwarded").
- **Share button in every app**: share an Edit project, Office document or deck, Design page or chat message to people and groups as a **share card** that opens the exact item. Sharing an Edit project also gives those people access in Edit.
- People list = all HR staff plus anyone who has signed in, with profile photos. Each person sees only their own chats.

**Benefits**
- Cost: team chat without a paid chat tool.
- Control: office conversations and files stay on Limelight's own PC, not on personal WhatsApp.
- Time: one click from a share card to the right project, file or design - no hunting for links.

**Why it matters**
- Client work, drafts and feedback stop being scattered across personal phones and groups.

### Create

- One Create page with an Image / Video switch.
- **AI Image**: prompt, style (Photo, Cinematic, Poster, 3D…), size (1:1, 4:5, 9:16, 16:9), Fast or Best, 1, 2 or 4 pictures, and "Leave out" (things to avoid).
- Pictures are made by Limelight AI on Limelight's own PCs and saved to the Library.
- Open Create straight from search with the typed text.

**Benefits**
- Cost: no per-image fees or paid image-AI subscriptions.
- Speed: social sizes (square, 4:5, story/reel, widescreen) in one step.
- Control: prompts and pictures never leave the office.

**Why it matters**
- Quick concept visuals and backgrounds for posts without sending ministry briefs to an outside AI service.

### Library

- **Videos tab**: a YouTube-style player for **finished Edit renders** - title, project, who rendered it, date and size.
- Buttons: **Download**, **Share** (to people/groups in Chats), **Open in Edit**.
- Searchable list (by render, project or person), newest first, with thumbnails; **Autoplay next**; taller player for 9:16 reels.
- Each person sees only the renders Edit allows them to see.
- **Images tab**: the AI image library.

**Benefits**
- Time: review finished videos in one place without downloading files or digging through folders.
- Control: access follows Edit's own rules.
- Quality: easy side-by-side review of versions before delivery.

**Why it matters**
- Account and client teams can watch and share the latest cut the moment it is rendered.

### Profile

- **Overview**: photo, role, department, employee ID, joining date, shift; this month's present / late / leave / absent; leave balance and own leaves.
- **Attendance** and **Leaves / WFH** pages from the HR app.
- **Time**: staff see their own clock in/out; **admins** see any day's clock in/out (with "late"), active work time per app and task time, and can open a person's sessions and tasks (time taken vs planned, overruns in red).
- Automatic **work-time log**: mouse/keyboard activity per app, session ends after 5 minutes idle.
- **Team** directory (about 54 people) with photo and a Message button.
- Only safe fields are shown: **no salary, bank details, ID numbers or phone**.

**Benefits**
- Time: staff check attendance and leave without asking HR.
- Control: admins see real work time per app and task overruns.
- Privacy: sensitive HR fields are never shown on profiles.

**Why it matters**
- Gives managers a fair, data-based view of workload on deadline-heavy accounts.

### Edit (video editor)

- Opens inside the workspace **with no second sign-in**; Edit's own project tabs show inside.
- The team's live editor runs on Limelight's own GPU server (renders with 4 workers, Whisper speech-to-text, NVENC GPU encoding), with a **standby server** that takes over if the main one stops.
- Latest release: **Audio tab with Beat Maker**, per-person presets, templates and music, "Ask Limelight AI" in Flow.
- **Open in Premiere / DaVinci** through the Windows app: it first copies all the timeline's footage to the PC, then opens the project with nothing offline; re-opening copies only changes.
- Share a project from the workspace and it grants Edit access too.
- Finished renders appear in Library → Videos.
- Each Edit team member gets a project folder on the NAS.
- Updates are tested automatically, tried on the standby PC first, and taken by the live server only when no render is running.

**Benefits**
- Time: no separate login; footage collection for Premiere/DaVinci is automatic.
- Quality: GPU rendering and a safe release flow that never interrupts a running render.
- Control: projects and footage stay on Limelight's own servers and NAS.

**Why it matters**
- Video is Limelight's core deliverable; the editor is shared by the whole team, keeps working if one server goes down, and hands off cleanly to professional tools when needed.

### Design (Limelight Studio)

- Home: new design in px, mm, inch or feet; 12 size presets; templates ("Design gallery"); your designs with trash.
- **File tabs** like Photoshop windows and **multiple artboards** per file (for example a post and a story in one file).
- Five tabs at the bottom:
  - **Design** (Photoshop + Canva): full Photoshop-style toolbar, menus, shortcuts, Layers panel (groups, masks, blend modes, Drop Shadow, Smart Filters), Select and Mask, templates, Hindi fonts, photo frames, brand panel.
  - **Vector** (Illustrator / CorelDRAW): Bezier pen, shapes, anchors, Pathfinder, clipping masks, Image Trace, SVG import/export.
  - **Book** (InDesign + QuarkXPress): margins, columns, spreads, master pages with page numbers and running heads, linked text boxes, Autoflow, text styles incl. Hindi. A 300-paragraph story flows across about 12 pages in about 1.6 s.
  - **3D** (Blender-style): shapes, extruded Limelight mark, gold/chrome/glass/neon materials, lights, shadows; insert into the design as a layer.
  - **Color** (Lightroom Develop-style): non-destructive GPU photo editing - presets, history, before/after, curves, colour mixer and grading, detail, effects.
- **Generative Fill and Expand**: select an area and type a prompt for 3 variations on a new layer; "Remove" works in about 1 s without AI; Add/Expand in about 30 s on Limelight's own AI.
- **Exports**: PNG, JPG, SVG, PDF, **layered PSD** (named layers, folders, masks, editable text), and **AI, CDR and vector PDF** through CorelDRAW on the server PC (real text, Hindi editable); "All formats" as one ZIP. PSD and SVG import.
- **Live editing**: two people on one design see each other's changes, selections and pointers.
- Opens with the workspace login; no separate account.

**Benefits**
- Cost: one tool covers much of the daily work otherwise done across several paid design apps.
- Time: one design file holds every size; one click exports all formats for printers and vendors.
- Quality: printers and vendors get real, editable PSD/AI/CDR files with Hindi text intact.
- Control: designs and AI fills stay on Limelight's own PCs.

**Why it matters**
- Ministry campaigns need the same creative in many sizes and formats, often in Hindi and English, fast - this is built for exactly that.

### Office

**Work**
- **Dashboard**: staff get their open, overdue and due-today tasks, a score ring with rank and on-time %, one-click next steps, leaderboard and active projects. Admins get who is in, overdue tasks, approvals waiting, OT to approve, team hours, projects at risk and a departments table.
- **Mail**: team mail kept on Limelight's own server.
- **Calendar**: events with team invites and RSVP, repeats, colours, 10-minute reminder, optional video room.
- **Meetings**: in-app video calls with camera, mic, screen share, raise hand and chat.
- **Tasks**: six-column board (To do → On hold), drag to change status, Mine / Assigned by me / Everyone, quick task.
- **Projects**: completion ring, client, Billable chip, hours against estimate (red when over).
- **Files**: each person's own folder on the office NAS.

**Create**
- **Document / Grid / Deck**: Word, Excel and PowerPoint-style editors that save **real .docx, .xlsx and .pptx** files (PDF too, Hindi OK), with **live co-editing** and Share. Usable on a phone.

**Team and HR**
- Staff, Departments, Events (birthdays, anniversaries, holidays), News, Notifications (grouped by day), HR Settings - all restyled into the workspace.

**Overtime**
- Staff log OT once a day; it is checked automatically against clock-in/clock-out.
- Admins approve, change, reject or undo; decided entries are locked.
- Approved OT flows into Salary and replaces the HR app's automatic OT, so nothing is paid twice.

**Salary (admins and owners only)**
- Month's pay for everyone from attendance and payroll: absences, half days, extra leave, late marks (every 3 = ½ day), OT pay and adjustments.
- Editable rules, totals and CSV download. Staff are refused by the server.

**Attendance (admins and owners only)**
- Everyone's month day by day (present, WFH, half day, absent, leave, holiday, late), with totals and CSV.

**Leave approvals (admins and owners only)**
- Pending leave, half-day and WFH requests with each person's leave balance; Approve or Reject with a note, and the person is told.

**Admin pages**: Payroll, Payments, Clients, Reports, Performance (admins only).

**Benefits**
- Cost: internal mail, calendar, meetings, files and office editors without per-seat cloud subscriptions.
- Time: OT checked against the clock automatically; salary worked out from attendance in one page instead of a spreadsheet.
- Control: each person has their own Office account and files; mail and files stay on Limelight's own server and NAS.
- Quality: files stay real Office files any client or ministry can open.

**Why it matters**
- Day-to-day admin (tasks, approvals, overtime, pay) is where small agencies lose hours; this puts it next to the creative work and the HR data it depends on.

### Limelight AI

- Private AI that runs **only on Limelight's own PCs**, with **no cloud API**.
- **Ask**: chat in Hindi, English or Hinglish; attach PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and photos; make and edit pictures in the chat.
- **Work**: an office agent that reads documents and writes .docx and .xlsx inside a folder, makes images and keeps a task list, with Ask-first / Auto / Full-auto permission modes.
- **Build**: a coding agent that reads and edits files and runs commands and tests.
- **Images**: image studio and gallery - Fast (about 10 s) and Quality (about 41 s); picture edit (about 64 s); Hindi/English text added as a clean layer in 2-3 s.
- Replies in the user's own language; no invented facts or numbers; voice typing.
- Model picker with plain names (Fastest, Deep thinking, Best for code, Best for photos); Fastest writes about 107-111 tokens (word pieces) per second.
- **Per-person chats**: staff see only their own conversations. No extra login inside the workspace.

**Benefits**
- Cost: no per-seat AI subscriptions or per-image fees.
- Control: confidential client briefs and documents never leave the office.
- Time: drafts, summaries, Excel/Word output and visuals in one place.
- Quality: Hindi text on images is drawn correctly as a separate layer.

**Why it matters**
- Government material cannot safely go to public AI services; this gives the team AI help without that risk.

## 4. Platform-wide features

- **One login for every app**: staff sign in once with their existing HR email and password; Edit, Design, Office, HR pages and Limelight AI open already signed in. No new accounts in any app.
- **Sessions last 30 days from last use**; logout ends a session at once.
- **Works on phone, tablet and computer**: bottom tab bar on phones, slide to clock in, Office pages and the Document / Grid / Deck editors adapted for small screens.
- **Installable app**: add to the home screen on Android and iPhone, install on Mac and Windows - no app store needed. A "No connection" page shows when offline.
- **Windows app**: a frameless Limelight window that prefers the office network for speed, handles "Open in Premiere / DaVinci", and updates itself from the NAS.
- **Fast loading**: every file link carries an automatic version stamp, so files cache for a year but updates reach every device at once; a stylesheet that took 2.1 s now takes about 0.45 s and is not refetched on repeat visits.
- **Own servers**: everything runs on Limelight's own PCs and NAS. Data stays in-house, there are no per-seat SaaS fees for these tools, and office PCs use the office network first.
- **Standby / failover**: if the main video editor server stops, the standby takes over; if the main AI server is busy or down, a second PC can answer; app status is re-checked every 20 s.
- **English interface**: all visible text is English (Limelight AI and the Office agent still reply in the user's language).
- **Security**:
  - Roles: **owners**, **admins** and **staff**; admin pages and Salary are refused for staff by the server.
  - Salary, bank details, ID numbers and phone are never shown on profiles.
  - Apps trust the workspace only through shared keys; if a key is missing, the app refuses instead of letting someone in as another person.
  - HTTPS only on the public site; secure, HttpOnly session cookie; saved HR tokens encrypted.
  - Each person has their own Office account, files and AI chats.
  - Anything public sits behind the workspace login.

## 5. Overall benefits and importance

### Paid tools it replaces (fully or partly)

| Paid tool | Covered by | How much |
|---|---|---|
| Slack, Microsoft Teams chat, WhatsApp work groups | Chats + Share cards | **Mostly** for internal team chat (no outside contacts) |
| Google Workspace (Gmail, Calendar, Meet, Drive) | Office Mail, Calendar, Meetings, Files | **Partly** - internal team mail only (no outside mailbox today); camera calls between two PCs not yet tested live |
| Microsoft 365 (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneDrive) | Document / Grid / Deck, Files | **Partly** - staff need no per-seat licence for these editors, but files are saved through Microsoft Office installed on the server PC |
| Adobe Photoshop | Design tab | **Partly** - no adjustment layers, Liquify or RAW; generative fill is slower and softer |
| Adobe Illustrator / CorelDRAW (drawing) | Vector tab | **Partly** - CDR/AI export still uses the CorelDRAW installed on the server PC |
| Adobe InDesign / QuarkXPress | Book tab | **Partly** - books, masters, linked text, Hindi styles |
| Adobe Lightroom | Color tab | **Partly** - no crop, healing, masking or RAW |
| Canva (paid) | Design templates, sizes, brand panel, Create | **Mostly** for social sizes and templates |
| Frame.io / review links | Library videos + Share | **Partly** - watch, download and share renders; no frame comments |
| ChatGPT / Copilot / Midjourney (team seats) | Limelight AI + Create | **Partly** - smaller local models; no video generation yet |
| Time-tracking tools | Profile Time + work-time log | **Partly** - work time per app and task time; not linked to attendance |
| Spreadsheet payroll and OT registers | Overtime + Salary | **Mostly** - checked against attendance; not yet checked against real payroll |

Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve are **not** replaced: Edit hands projects to them cleanly.

### Value in plain terms

- Every tool above is normally paid **per person, per month**. With about 54 staff, even a few such subscriptions add up to a large recurring yearly cost. The workspace replaces part of that with hardware Limelight already owns.
- Paid AI is often charged per seat or per image; Limelight AI and Create have no usage fees.
- The real costs that remain: the office PCs and NAS, electricity and internet, Microsoft Office and CorelDRAW installed on the server PC, and the time to maintain it.

### Control of client data

- Ministry briefs, drafts, footage, designs, mail, chats and AI prompts stay on Limelight's own PCs and NAS.
- No client material is sent to an outside AI service.
- Access follows one HR account per person; when someone leaves HR, they lose the workspace too.

### Speed of delivery

- Brief → AI image → design in every size → export all formats, or footage → edit → render → Library → share, without changing app or login.
- Share cards, one search and live co-editing cut the time spent sending files and links.
- Failover keeps editing and AI running when one server is down.

### One place for the team

- Chat, tasks, attendance, overtime, salary, files and every creative tool sit behind one login, on any device.
- Managers see workload, time and approvals in one dashboard.
